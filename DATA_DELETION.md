# Data deletion

ALIS runs no account system and does not build profiles or collect personal data about visitors
or followers. Apart from information people voluntarily send us, there is normally little or
nothing held about an individual.

## Asking ALIS to delete data

Email **fallintodusk@proton.me** and include:

- which platform and account the request concerns;
- a link to the relevant post or content, if there is one.

We verify the request, delete anything under our control that relates to you, and reply within
**30 days** saying what was deleted — or confirming that nothing was held.

See the full [ALIS Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for what is processed and why.
